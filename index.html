About

I'm a final-year PhD student in Computer Science in the AI-ML group at the Institute for Advancing Intelligence (IAI), TCG CREST, and an applied mathematician by training. I work on speaker diarization, a challenging research problem in which I apply deep learning and signal processing techniques to multi-speaker conversational speech recorded in noisy, real-life conditions. My broader research interests include unsupervised machine learning and explainability through causal inference.

I am jointly supervised by Swami Punyeshwarananda (RKMVERI, Belur) and Dr. Md Sahidullah (TCG CREST).

Before starting my PhD, I completed a B.Sc. (Hons.) in Mathematics at the University of Delhi and an M.Sc. in Applied Mathematics at the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Mandi. During my master's, I spent a semester as a DAAD scholar at the Communications Theory Lab, TU Dresden, Germany, where I worked on an ill-posed inverse problem in image processing.

My hobbies include teaching applied mathematics, playing soccer, trekking, and practising yoga and meditation.

Currently, I am actively looking for industrial research opportunities across the broad applications of speech and audio AI.
