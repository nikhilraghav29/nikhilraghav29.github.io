About

I'm a PhD scholar in computer science at the Institute for Advancing Intelligence, TCG CREST, working at the intersection of deep learning, signal processing and conversational AI. My research focuses on building robust speaker diarization systems, a foundational capability for modern conversational AI systems.

I am jointly supervised by Swami Punyeshwarananda and Dr. Md Sahidullah.

By training I am an applied mathematician, with an M.Sc. from IIT Mandi and a B.Sc. (Hons.) in Mathematics from the University of Delhi. During my master's, I was a semester exchange student at TU Dresden.

Beyond research, I practise yoga, play soccer and badminton, and make educational content for the Vigyan Bharat YouTube channel on mathematics.
